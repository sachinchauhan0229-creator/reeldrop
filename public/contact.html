Contact ReelDrop

For support, privacy questions or copyright concerns, contact Sachin Chauhan at sachinchauhan0229@gmail.com.

Report a download problem

Include the public Instagram link, the exact error message, and the browser and device you used. Check our troubleshooting guide first. Do not send passwords, session cookies, private account details or API credentials.

Copyright concerns

Identify the work and the specific public URL involved, explain your relationship to the rights holder, and provide a way to contact you. ReelDrop does not host a permanent catalogue of Instagram videos.

Privacy requests

Describe the request and the page or interaction involved. Send only the information needed to understand the issue. Email you send will be handled to respond to your request; your email provider may process the message under its own policies.
