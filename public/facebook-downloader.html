How to download a Facebook video or Reel

  1. Open a public video or Reel in Facebook and choose Share → Copy link.
  2. Paste the link here and press Find media.
  3. Choose a quality if available and press Download. Your browser saves the MP4.

If a share link fails, open it and copy the full video or Reel address. Some videos require a Facebook login or are unavailable from the public web. Quality and audio depend on the source file.

Save only videos you own or have permission to download.
