A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Everything you need before you drop a link.

What is an Instagram video downloader?

It takes a public post URL and retrieves video links directly using open-source software, so you can save content you have permission to download.

Can I download from a private account?

No. ReelDrop is designed for public posts. Do not enter your Instagram password here.

Does it work on my phone?

The interface works in modern mobile and desktop browsers. File saving depends on your browser and the returned media link.

Which file formats and quality are available?

Available formats and quality depend on the original video and what Instagram makes accessible. ReelDrop does not upscale media.

Is ReelDrop connected to Instagram?

No. ReelDrop is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
